The full job, not half of it

Most landlord tools cover either finding tenants or collecting rent. LandlordDesk covers the whole run — list the vacancy, screen the applicant, build the lease, collect the rent, fix the sink, and hand your accountant a clean Schedule E.

💵
Rent

Rent tracking & arrears

A monthly tracker for every unit — who paid, how much, when, and how. The arrears table shows exactly who owes what, for which month, and how many days late, based on each lease's due day. Full payment history across every month, always one click away.

📝
Applications

Applications + screening records

Every application in one pipeline — New, Under review, Approved, Declined — with income, occupants, pets, employer, and rental history on file. Track each screening check (credit, criminal, eviction) with its status, cost, and your provider's result, then approve and convert the applicant into a tenant in one click.

📄
Leases

Lease builder

Pick the unit and the lease builds itself — rent, deposit, tenant, and term prefilled from your records. Set the late fee and grace period, toggle pets, smoking, and utility splits, add your house rules, and print a clean lease with signature lines ready. Saved leases stay re-printable.

🔑
Tenant view

A window for your tenants

See exactly what your tenant sees: their balance broken down by month, what's due and when, their lease details, and the documents you've shared with them. Tenants submit maintenance requests that land straight in your maintenance list — no more repair requests buried in text messages.

🗂️
Documents

Document storage

Leases, letters, receipts, ID copies — attach any file to the property, unit, or tenant it belongs to, and flag it shared when the tenant should see it. Receipts attach directly to the expense they prove. Everything is where the record is.

🏦
Money

Bank import & per-property P&L

Import a CSV from your bank or QuickBooks and file each transaction into the right property and category with one click — no retyping statements. See profit and loss per property for any month, a year-end summary by category, and where every dollar of repairs, taxes, and utilities went.

⏰
Late fees

Late fees & reminders, handled

When rent runs 5+ days late, LandlordDesk suggests the exact late fee from that tenant's lease — one click applies it and records it. The reminders queue writes the friendly reminder or the firm late notice for every tenant who owes, ready to copy into a text or email. Polite, consistent, and never forgotten again.

🧾
Taxes

Schedule E export

At tax time, export a per-property Schedule E summary — rents received and expenses already mapped to the IRS lines (repairs, insurance, utilities, taxes) — printable or as a CSV your accountant will actually thank you for.

🔧
Maintenance

Maintenance & vendors

Every request tracked by property, priority, and status, with the vendor assigned and the cost recorded — so "what did that plumbing call run me this year?" is a lookup, not a memory test. Your vendor directory (plumber, electrician, HVAC) feeds the picker, so the right number is always attached to the job.

Honest answers

We'd rather tell you exactly where things stand than oversell. Here's the straight version.

Where does my data live?

In this demo build, your data is stored in your own browser, on your own device — nothing is uploaded anywhere, and there's no account to create. You can export a complete backup of everything (JSON) or a rent roll (CSV) at any time from the Notes & Data tab. The founder launch adds proper accounts with cloud sync, so your data follows you between phone and computer with automatic backups.

Can tenants pay rent through LandlordDesk?

In the demo, the tenant view shows the tenant's balance and records payments into your rent tracker, so you can see exactly how the flow works — but no real money moves yet. Live online rent payments (ACH/card, with tenant autopay) arrive with the hosted launch, alongside accounts and cloud sync.

Does it run credit and background checks?

LandlordDesk tracks screening for every applicant — credit, criminal, and eviction checks, each with its status, cost, and result recorded from your screening provider — and keeps that record attached to the application permanently. Ordering reports from a bureau inside the app is a launch integration; today you order from your provider as usual and record the result here.

What exactly is the "founder launch"?

LandlordDesk is being launched by its founder, in the open. The product you can try right now is complete and working as a single-device tool. The hosted launch adds the pieces that need servers: accounts and cloud sync, live rent payments, in-app screening orders, automatic reminder sending, and state lease packs with e-signature. Founding members lock in the launch ladder — four flat plans from $49.99 to $299/month — and no plan is ever priced per unit.

Will the inspection checklist guarantee I pass my Section 8 inspection?

No — and be wary of any tool that promises that. The checklist is a preparation walk-through modeled on common housing-quality (HQS-style) inspection items: it helps you find and fix the things inspectors commonly cite, before they cite them, and gives each property a readiness score. The official inspection is still the inspector's call.

Is the price really flat — what happens when I buy more units?

Really flat. Every plan is one fixed monthly price for you, the landlord — never a charge per unit, per property, or per tenant. Plans are sized by portfolio (Desk up to 10 units, Pro up to 25, Portfolio up to 50, Empire unlimited), so your bill only changes when you choose to move up a plan — never automatically because you bought another door. Annual billing on any plan costs 10 months, so two months are free.
