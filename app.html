Rent & Payments

Mark each unit's rent for the month, record when and how it was paid, and see exactly who is behind — and by how much.

Arrears

Every occupied unit that still owes rent — current month (once the due day has passed) plus any month with a recorded shortfall. When rent is 5+ days late and the lease has a late fee, LandlordDesk suggests the fee — apply it with one click and it is recorded under Money → Other income.

Payment history

Every payment recorded in the tracker, newest first.

Reminders queue

Ready-to-send messages for everyone who owes rent, written from their actual balance and due date. Copy one and paste it into your text or email app. (Automatic sending by text/email arrives with the full hosted version.)

Properties & Units

Add your properties, then add a unit to each one — rent, deposit, due day, size, and status. Link a tenant from the Tenants tab (or right in the unit form).

Vacancy listings

For every vacant or listed unit, generate a ready-to-post listing from the unit's own facts — headline and full description you can copy straight into Zillow, Apartments.com, Craigslist, or Facebook Marketplace. (Automatic syndication feeds arrive with the full hosted version.)

Tenants

One profile per tenant — contact details, lease dates, deposit held, and the late fee in their lease. Leases ending within 60 days are highlighted.

Other income

Money that isn't rent — late fees, laundry, parking, application fees, and anything else. It counts in the P&L and the year-end summary.

Bank statement import

Download a CSV from your bank (or export one from QuickBooks) and import it here. Each transaction lands in the list below — pick a category and click Categorize to file it into your expenses or other income. Rows you have not categorized yet are flagged so nothing gets lost.

Year-end summary (tax view)

Schedule E summary

Your rents and expenses for the year, grouped the way IRS Schedule E (Form 1040) asks for them — one block per property, ready to hand to your tax preparer. Print it or download the CSV. This is a bookkeeping summary in Schedule E format, not a filed tax form.

Rental Applications

Log every application you receive, track screening for each applicant, and convert an approved applicant into a tenant with one click. Screening results are recorded here from whichever screening provider you use — live credit, criminal, and eviction reports ordered inside LandlordDesk arrive with the full hosted version.

Lease Builder

Fill in the terms once and LandlordDesk generates a clean, printable lease document with blank signature lines. The document records the state you select so the right state-specific template pack can be applied at signing — state packs and e-signature arrive with the full hosted version, and any lease should be reviewed by a local attorney before use.

Saved leases

Tenant View

A preview of the tenant portal — this is what your tenant sees for their unit: what they owe, a pay-now button, a way to submit maintenance requests (they land in your Maintenance tab), their lease details, and documents you have shared with them. In this prototype everything happens on this device; the live portal with online payments arrives with the full hosted version.

Documents

Leases, inspection letters, ID copies, receipts — attach files to a property, a unit, or a tenant and keep them with the record. Files are stored in this browser on this device as part of your LandlordDesk data, so keep individual files modest in size (photos and PDFs under ~1–2 MB work best). Mark a document "shared" to make it visible in the Tenant View.

Tasks & Reminders

Lease renewals, inspection dates, repairs you promised — anything with a due date. Overdue items are flagged in red, here and on the Dashboard.

Vendors & Contacts

Your plumber, electrician, HVAC tech, handyman — everyone you call, in one directory. Vendors you add here can be assigned to maintenance requests.

Property Notes

A written log per property — inspector comments, utility account numbers, quirks of the house, anything you'd otherwise forget. (To attach files like leases and photos, use the Documents tab.)

Backup & export

Your data lives in this browser on this device. Export a backup file regularly — it contains everything (properties, tenants, payments, money, maintenance, tasks, notes, checklists) and can be imported back here at any time. The rent roll downloads as a spreadsheet-ready CSV.
Importing replaces everything currently shown with the backup file's contents — export first if you're unsure.
